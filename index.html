Privacy Policy – Interval

Effective Date: November 19, 2026
Last Updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Workout Interval Timer (“the App”), a free interval workout timer application developed by Coder Nii-Chan.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use its features.

Interval does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to access its workout timer functionality.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Interval does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials through its workout features.

The App does not operate its own server or maintain a cloud database for storing users’ workout routines.

However, certain information may be processed by third-party services, particularly Google AdMob, as explained in Section 4.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Interval allows users to create, customize, and save workout routines and preferences.

The following information may be stored locally on your device:

  • Workout and exercise names.
  • Saved workout routines and presets.
  • Workout duration and rest interval settings.
  • Number of rounds and other timer preferences.

This information is stored locally using the device’s application storage and is not transmitted to servers operated by the developer.

You can remove this information by deleting individual routines, clearing the App’s storage, or uninstalling the App.

Please note that uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may permanently delete locally stored workout information.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Interval uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements within the App.

Google AdMob may collect or process certain information automatically when advertisements are requested, displayed, or interacted with.

Depending on your device, advertising settings, location, and applicable consent requirements, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers.
  • IP addresses and general device or network information.
  • Information about advertisements displayed and advertising interactions.
  • App-related diagnostics and information used to measure advertising performance.

Google may use this information to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising services, subject to applicable laws and user preferences.

For additional information, please review Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Advertising Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Interval does not use separate third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

The developer does not operate an independent analytics platform or crash-reporting server to monitor individual users’ workout activities.

This does not exclude technical information that may be processed by Google AdMob or other platform services as described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Information Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or independently distribute users’ personal information.

Workout routines and preferences stored locally are not transmitted to the developer’s servers.

However, information may be processed by Google and its service providers as necessary to provide advertising functionality and related services.

Such processing is governed by the applicable third-party privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Workout routines and preferences remain stored locally on your device until you delete them, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App.

The developer does not maintain a separate cloud database containing users’ workout routines or personal accounts.

Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google’s applicable data retention, processing, and deletion policies.

For questions regarding information processed by Google, please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy.

8. Children’s Privacy

Interval does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through its workout features.

The App’s advertising configuration and distribution are subject to applicable Google Play policies, Google AdMob requirements, and relevant privacy laws.

9. Data Security

Interval is designed to minimize the information handled by the developer.

Workout routines and preferences are stored locally on the user’s device rather than on developer-operated servers.

We encourage users to maintain appropriate security on their devices. Third-party services used by the App maintain their own security practices and are responsible for their respective data-processing activities.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App’s functionality, advertising services, applicable laws, or privacy requirements.

Any revisions will be published on this webpage, together with an updated effective or last-updated date.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
